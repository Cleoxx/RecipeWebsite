Easy basic recipe, that can be used for most of your favorite desserts.

Ingredients:

- 6 eggs
- 180 g sugar
- 300 g flour
- 300 g margerine
- 1 pckg baking powder
- 100 ml milk

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees 
2. Mix the eggs and sugar until they are fully mixed and you can see small air bubbles on top. Add margarine and repeat.
3. Mix together and slowly add all dry ingredients (flour and baking powder) 
4. If the dough is too heavy, add some milk.
