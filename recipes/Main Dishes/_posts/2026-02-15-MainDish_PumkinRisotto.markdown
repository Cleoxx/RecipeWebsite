Best recipe for a delicious, creamy fall risotto.

Serves 4 - 6

ingredients:
- 1 pc Hokkaido pumpkin
- 1 pc onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- 250 g risotto rice
- 1L vegetable broth
- 250 mL white wine (dry)
- 250 g Parmesan
- Pumpkin seeds
- Salt, pepper

Instructions: 

1. Dice Hokkaido pumpkin. Then roast in oven for 30 min at 180°C (40 min without preheating)
2. Set 1/3 aside, mix 2/3 in a blender (use vegetable broth to blend)
3. Dice onions and garlic. In a deep pot, fry the onions in some oil (1-2 tbsp), add garlic and rice, keep stirring until the rice gets some color. Add the wine until the pot cools down. Add the rest of the broth, the blended pumpkin and the pumpkin dice, and cover the pot.
4. If possible (pot has no plastic handles), put the covered pot into the oven and leave for about 15 min (still at 180 degrees)
5. Check on the risotto and stir every 10 min, until the liquid is absorbed. When the liquid is absorbed and the rice is soft, take the pot out and add Parmesan. If the rice is still hard, add some more broth.
6. Let it cool down and serve with some more Parmesan on top. Add some pumpkin seeds for decoration.
